Mission 3 of 4

Vessels & Your Pulse

Blood travels through three kinds of tubes. Then — find your own heartbeat!

Vessel 1 of 3Which blood vessel am I?

❤️ Find your pulse — each tap you feel is one heartbeat pushing blood through an artery.

  1. Turn one hand palm up.
  2. Place two fingers (not your thumb) on your wrist, just below the thumb.
  3. Press gently until you feel a tap… tap… tap.
Can't find it? Try gently on the side of your neck, beside your windpipe.
15 s left — tap once for every beat you feel

The sound is paused so you can feel your own heart.

🚨 For grown-ups: know the warning signs of a heart attack

  • Chest pain, pressure or tightness
  • Pain spreading to the arm, back, neck, jaw or stomach
  • Shortness of breath, cold sweat, feeling sick or light-headed
